# 🚀 Nexora – Campus Opportunity Radar

> **Discover What's Next.**

Nexora is an AI-powered personalized opportunity discovery and recommendation platform designed to help students discover relevant internships, hackathons, competitions, workshops, scholarships, research opportunities, and campus events.

Instead of forcing students to search through scattered announcements across college notices, websites, WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn, and external platforms, Nexora organizes opportunity information and recommends the opportunities that best match each student's profile, skills, interests, eligibility, preferences, and deadlines.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students receive opportunity information from many disconnected sources:

- College notices
- Department websites
- Clubs and student communities
- WhatsApp and Telegram groups
- LinkedIn
- Internship platforms
- Hackathon platforms
- Emails

This creates several problems:

- Information overload
- Missed deadlines
- Eligibility confusion
- Skill mismatch
- Lack of personalization
- Poor application tracking

The real problem is not simply **finding opportunities**, but:

> **Finding the right opportunity for the right student at the right time.**

---

## 💡 Solution

Nexora converts scattered opportunity information into structured and personalized recommendations.

```text
Opportunity Sources
